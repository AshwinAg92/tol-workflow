Who we are

Devotional at heart, Bollywood when it counts.

Together, Out Loud started with a simple idea: the music at your event shouldn't just play in the background — it should be played, live, by people who feel it. What began as bhajan jamming sessions has grown into a full band that moves between kirtan, wedding pheras, satsangs and Bollywood sets, depending on what the room needs.

Led by Ashwin, who performs at every show we take on.
"Give us a harmonium, a tabla and a room full of people — we'll take care of the rest."

What we play

Five formats, one live band.

Bhajan Jamming

An intimate, high-energy bhajan session built around live harmonium, tabla and vocals — call-and-response kirtan that has the whole room singing along by the second song.

Bhajan Clubbing

The same devotional core, reimagined for the dance floor — a higher-tempo, club-style spin on bhajan jamming that keeps the energy up all night.

Musical Pheras

Live music woven through the wedding pheras themselves — a string-and-vocal ensemble that turns the walk around the fire into a performance the couple will actually remember.

Bollywood Jamming

A live band reimagining the songs everyone actually wants to hear — old classics and current chartbusters, played the way they were meant to be: live, loud, and together.

Devotional Satsang

A soulful evening of bhajans and devotional music, shaped for spiritual gatherings, temple functions and community satsangs of any size.

Planning a wedding day?

Musical Pheras pairs naturally with Bhajan Jamming for a wedding that's fully live, start to finish — ask us about the combo.

Shraddhanjali Satsang

For families who want to honour a loved one's memory with a gentle, respectful evening of devotional bhajans. Handled with the same care as everything else we do — just quieter.
