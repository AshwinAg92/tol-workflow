WHO WE ARE

We don't perform for you.
We perform with you.

Together, Out Loud is a spiritual musical experience founded by husband-wife duo Prakriti Modi (a full-time Chartered Accountant) and Ashwin Agarwal (an entrepreneur). Instead of playing to a crowd, we play with one — turning a room of strangers into people who sing, laugh, dance and connect, together. No egos, no outsiders, no toxicity. Just music, and everyone in it.

— PRAKRITI & ASHWIN, FOUNDERS

FAQS

Questions, answered.

How is Together, Out Loud different from a live band?

We're not a covers band playing at your event — we build the experience around the audience. Every set is designed for people to sing, participate and connect with each other, not just listen.

Can you perform internationally?

Yes — we've already taken Together, Out Loud on tour across Europe, alongside shows in multiple cities across India. Reach out with your location and dates and we'll work out the logistics together.

Do you travel?

Yes. We've performed across multiple cities in India and travel for events outside our base city too. Travel and accommodation are billed at actuals.

Can we customize songs?

Absolutely — for Musical Pheras especially, we can write and perform a custom song for the couple. Other formats can also be tailored to specific songs or themes on request.

How many artists perform?

Typically 5 to 7 live musicians, depending on the scale of the event. We'll confirm the exact lineup with you during planning.

What technical setup is required?

We bring our own instruments. Sound, lighting and stage setup can be arranged as add-ons, or we can work with your venue's existing setup — we'll sort out the details when you enquire.

Can we book private events?

Yes — Private Celebrations is one of our formats, built for birthdays, anniversaries, housewarmings and other intimate family events.
