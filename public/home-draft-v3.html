WHO WE ARE

We don't perform for you.
We perform with you.

Together, Out Loud is a spiritual musical experience founded by husband-wife duo Ashwin Agarwal (an entrepreneur) and Prakriti Modi (a full-time Chartered Accountant). Instead of playing to a crowd, we play with one — turning a room of strangers into people who sing, laugh, dance and connect, together. No egos, no outsiders, no toxicity. Just music, and everyone in it.

— ASHWIN & PRAKRITI, FOUNDERS

Our mission: building communities through music.
